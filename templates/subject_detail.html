How is this calculated?

Your habit rate is estimated as p = (A + 1) / (H + 2) (Laplace smoothing), giving p = {{ A|add:1 }} / {{ H|add:2 }} ≈ {{ risk.probability|floatformat:2 }}.

The remaining R = {{ att.r }} classes are modelled as Binomial({{ att.r }}, p). The probability of attending at least {{ att.must }} of them is computed exactly using the binomial PMF.
